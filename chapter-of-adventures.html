Tour Overview

Discover the beauty of Sri Lanka.

1

Kitulgala White Water Rafting

Navigate exciting Grade 3-5 rapids on the Kelani River through lush rainforest, or add canyoning: abseil waterfalls and slide natural chutes.

2

Sigiriya & Hot Air Balloons

Experience rural life with ox-cart rides, trek Pidurangala at sunrise, and float gently at dawn above jungles in a hot air balloon.

3

Hill Country Explorations

Ride the scenic train from Kandy to Ella, hike to Little Adam's Peak, zipline over valleys, and trek the dawn 9km loop at Horton Plains.

4

Wildlife Safaris

Jeep safaris in Yala spotting leopards, or witness huge elephant herds gathering at Kaudulla & Udawalawe reservoirs.

5

Coastal & Marine Activities

Whale watching in Mirissa, surfing in Weligama, water sports in Bentota, kitesurfing in Kalpitiya, and scuba diving in Hikkaduwa.

6

Rainforest & Culture

Guided walks in the UNESCO-listed Sinharaja Rainforest and hands-on authentic Sri Lankan cooking classes.

This itinerary can be customized.
