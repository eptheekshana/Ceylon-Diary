8-Day Spiritual Journey
Chapter of Ramayana
Tour Overview

A luxurious private tour tracing the legendary Ramayana Trail across Sri Lanka, where ancient mythology intertwines with sacred temples, mystical caves, and breathtaking landscapes. Follow the footsteps of Lord Rama, Sita, Hanuman, and Ravana through sites of devotion, captivity, battle, and triumph—blending profound spirituality with the island's natural beauty.

1

Munneswaram, Manavari & Madampe Temples

Ancient coastal shrines rich in Ramayana lore: Munneswaram and Manavari are revered Shiva temples where Lord Rama prayed to atone for defeating Ravana, while nearby Madampe Subrahmanya Temple honours Lord Murugan.

2

Koneswaram Temple (Trincomalee)

Iconic cliff-top Hindu temple dedicated to Lord Shiva, deeply linked to King Ravana's devotion; includes the revered Shakti Peetham and the nearby Ravana Hot Water Wells.

3

Sigiriya (Cobra Hood Cave)

Ancient rock fortress with caves associated with Sita's captivity. Also includes a visit to the Sigiriya Lion Rock Fortress.

4

Kandy

Visit the Aluviharaya Rock Cave, the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, enjoy a Kandy City tour and witness a Cultural dance.

5

Sri Bhaktha Hanuman Temple (Ramboda)

Hilltop temple honoring Hanuman's search for Sita.

6

Sita Amman Temple & Ashok Vatika

Serene site where Sita was held captive amid lush gardens, also featuring the Gayatri Peetham.

7

Divurumpola Temple

Location of Sita's Agni Pariksha (trial by fire) to prove her purity.

8

Ravana Ella Falls & Ravana Cave

Majestic waterfall and caves tied to Ravana's palace and hideouts.

9

Galle

Rumassala Sanjeevani Mountain, associated with the legendary mountain Hanuman brought.

10

Kelaniya Vibhishana Temple

Sites of Vibhishana's coronation and a unique five-faced Hanuman shrine in Colombo.
