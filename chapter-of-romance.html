07 Day Honeymoon Tour
Chapter of Romance & Relaxation
Tour Overview

A dreamy private chauffeur-driven escape for newlyweds, weaving coastal sunsets, misty hill country whispers, sacred cultural moments, and historic charm. Indulge in luxury boutique hideaways with private pools, candlelit dinners, couples' spa rituals, and intimate experiences amid Sri Lanka's most romantic landscapes.

1

Negombo

Gentle arrival and relaxation post-flight; unwind with lagoon sunsets and champagne toasts in tropical seclusion.

2

Hill Country (Kandy, Nuwara Eliya & Ella)

Journey into cool, misty heights; explore sacred Temple of the Tooth, stroll botanical gardens, wander "Little England" charm in Nuwara Eliya, scenic train ride to Ella, tea plantation walks, Horton Plains sunrise trek, and waterfall picnics.

3

Tea Country (Hatton/Castlereagh Area)

Immersive emerald tea estates; private plantation tours, factory visits, infinity pool sunsets, hand-in-hand walks, and secluded picnics overlooking rolling hills.

4

South Coast Beaches (Bentota to Tangalle via Mirissa & Unawatuna)

Finale of pristine sands; relaxation, gentle water sports, seasonal whale watching, sunset strolls, and private beachfront dinners in ultimate seclusion.

This itinerary is entirely tailored to your budget, travel dates, interests, and dream destinations, whether you prefer more relaxation, cultural immersion, adventure, or a perfect balance. For additional inspiring locations and exciting activities, explore our other chapters.
