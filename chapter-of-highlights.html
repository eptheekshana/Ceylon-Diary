Chapter of Highlights
Tour Overview

A comprehensive journey showcasing the cultural triangle, hill country, southern beaches and wildlife. Experience the very best of Sri Lanka in 14 days.

1

Negombo

Vibrant coastal gateway with golden beaches, bustling fish markets, serene lagoon boat rides, historic Dutch canals and St. Mary's Church.

2

Sigiriya Lion Rock

Dawn ascent to the legendary 5th-century rock fortress, an eighth wonder of the world.

3

Dambulla

Golden cave temples adorned with exquisite Buddhist art.

4

Sacred City Anuradhapura

The ancient ruins of Anuradhapura, the first capital of Ceylon.

5

Polonnaruwa

The majestic ruins of the second capital of ancient Ceylon.

6

Habarana / Kaudulla

Elephant gatherings in pristine national parks.

7

Kandy

Sacred Temple of the Tooth Relic and serene royal gardens.

8

Nuwara Eliya

Misty tea plantations and colonial-era charm in the cool hill country.

9

Ella

The iconic Nine Arch Bridge set amid lush jungles and scenic train rides.

10

Yala

Safari thrills tracking elusive leopards and wild elephants.

11

Southern Coast

Historic Dutch fort, golden beaches, and sunset walks along ancient ramparts.

12

Colombo City

Vibrant capital finale with modern flair and cultural contrasts.

This itinerary can be extended to include the East Coast (see the East Coast section), adventure activities (see the Adventures section), or any other preferred destinations or experiences. The duration can also be shortened by excluding selected destinations, as per your preference and budget.
